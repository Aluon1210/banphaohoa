## Users

Two audiences, confirmed by the owner:

- Households and small hosts in TP. Huế and TP. Đà Nẵng buying legal Z121 consumer fireworks for holidays, openings, and family events.
- Event organizers who need a site survey, a show designed to a budget, and technicians to run it safely.

Both are deciding whether to call, visit a store, or request a quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Routes: home and `/san-pham/[slug]` for the six catalog entries named in the brief.
- Quote form: required name and phone/Zalo, product dropdown kept in sync when a visitor chooses “Nhận Báo Giá”, note for quantity and delivery address. On submit, an on-screen confirmation plus hotline `0395 952 325`. No backend.
- Do not invent burn times, burst heights, prices, or permit numbers that are not on the product label or in the capability profile.
- Do not use military insignia as decoration. “Bộ Quốc phòng” and “Z121” stay as factual names.
- Imagery comes from `app/HSNL - SALAGARDEN.pdf` when an image can be extracted; otherwise a brand frame, not a fake photograph.

## Product Principles

- Lead with who is allowed to sell the firework and where to buy or book it.
- Put the phone number and the quote form within reach of every product.
- Show legal identity beside the sales pitch: tax code, representative, stores, warehouse.
- Safety copy stays at label-and-distance level. No composition, loading, or modification guidance.
- Prefer a photograph from the dossier over an invented picture.
